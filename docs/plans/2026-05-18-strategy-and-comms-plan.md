On your "done = answerable questions" framing — I think that's the right lens. The existing FAQs are mostly architectural (what goes where, who owns what). What's missing are operational
questions:

- What does a week of 2R content look like?
- How do we decide what to post vs what to hold back?
- What does success look like in 3 months? (metrics, even loose ones)
- What's the one thing YoYo/Sylvie should do first thing Monday?

On the issue tree — the SCQA in motivation.md gets to Q1 (architecture) and Q2 (identity). The issue tree branch that's underdeveloped is Q3: execution — once we know what 2R is and what
channels it uses, what do we actually produce and at what cadence?
